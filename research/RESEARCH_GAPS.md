## Free and current-content caveats

- A public video/course is not a guarantee of unrestricted regional playback or free cloud/API/GPU execution.
- Cloud free tiers, monthly training credits, trials and promoted bootcamps do not qualify as unlimited free core labs.
- Paid inference examples must be replaced with local/fake adapters for the no-spend core; those fixtures prove service controls, not model quality.
- Current MCP material is dated 2026-07-28; older videos may describe incompatible lifecycle/transport/SDK behavior.
- Both observed OpenAI Evals/Datasets guides carry the same October 31/November 30, 2026 deprecation timeline. Neither is used as a default hosted evaluation dependency.
- Older Docker/Argo/Terraform/Linux/provider videos are conceptual companions. Commands, credentials, licensing and service behavior must be checked against current docs.
- Architecture design plus local simulation is not proof of production operation or successful real cloud migration.

## Conditions before stronger completion claims

Do not label the package “exhaustive,” “all videos tested,” “representative market demand,” “all employers researched,” or “job-ready in fourteen days.” Those claims require evidence not obtained here. Schema validation and resolved IDs make the package application-readable; a future application must preserve these limitations instead of suppressing them.
